About the journal

Aims and scope

A venue for engineering research that can be checked, reproduced and put to work on real infrastructure and environmental problems.

CEER Journal is a peer-reviewed, open-access journal covering civil and environmental engineering. We publish research articles, review papers, case studies and short technical notes, with equal weight on method and on evidence from the field or laboratory.

Topics we publish

  • Structural engineering, earthquake and wind engineering, bridge and building retrofit
  • Geotechnical engineering, ground improvement, slopes, foundations and tunnelling
  • Water resources, hydrology, hydraulics, flood risk and groundwater
  • Environmental engineering, water and wastewater treatment, solid waste, air quality
  • Transportation and pavement engineering
  • Construction management, digital construction and project delivery
  • Sustainable and low-carbon materials, life-cycle assessment
  • Climate adaptation and infrastructure resilience

What reviewers look for

Technical soundness comes first. A paper does not need to be fashionable, but its data, assumptions and limitations must be stated clearly enough for another engineer to repeat or challenge the work. Negative and inconclusive results are welcome when the study was well designed.

How a paper moves through review

Review takes about 8 days, and publication follows about 2 days after acceptance.

  1. Submission check
    Scope, formatting, similarity screening, completeness of author details.
    ≈ 1 day
  2. Editor screening
    The handling editor decides whether the paper goes to review.
    ≈ 1 day
  3. Peer review
    Independent reviewers assess method, evidence and clarity.
    4–5 days
  4. Decision
    Accept, revise or reject, with reviewer comments. First decision in about 8 days in total.
    ≈ 1 day
  5. Revision
    Authors respond point by point; the editor may return it to reviewers.
    as agreed
  6. Publication
    Typesetting, article ID and DOI assigned, paper published online.
    ≈ 2 days

Format

  • A4 page, single column, 11 pt serif font, 1.5 line spacing, numbered lines.
  • Order: title, abstract, keywords, introduction, methods, results, discussion, conclusions, data availability, references.
  • Title page with author details is uploaded separately. The main file must be anonymous.
  • SI units throughout. Define every symbol where it first appears.
  • Equations numbered in parentheses on the right.

Figures and tables

  • Figures at 300 dpi or vector, with axis titles and units. Do not rely on colour alone.
  • Tables are editable text, not images. Each has a caption above it.
  • State the source of any figure or data that is not your own and confirm permission.

References

Use APA 7th edition author–date style. Cite standards and codes with their issuing body, number and year, for example IS 456:2000 or Eurocode 2.

Data and code

Provide the data behind each figure, or explain why you cannot. Numerical models should list software, version, mesh or grid size, boundary conditions and calibration data.

Ethics and originality

  • Similarity screening is applied to every submission. Overlap above 15% excluding references prompts a query to the authors.
  • Authorship is limited to people who made a substantial contribution and approved the final text.
  • Disclose funding and conflicts of interest. Field work needs the landowner or agency permissions noted.
  • Publication ethics follow the principles set out by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).

Frequently asked questions

Is there a fee to publish?

Yes. Authors from India pay ₹700 for publication plus ₹600 for the DOI, ₹1,300 in total. International authors pay USD 12 for publication plus USD 8 for the DOI, USD 20 in total.

How long does review take?

Review is quick: a decision in about 8 days, and publication about 2 days after that.

Do you accept conference extensions?

Yes, if the journal paper adds substantial new work and cites the conference version.
